Two corrections

The economy gate was never green

I reported it green after the materials tier. It was not: the suite was crashing at isRoadTile is not defined before it reached the rounds the tier breaks, and I read the truncated output as a pass.

An agent fixed that crash. With the suite running to completion it reports 12 failures across 4 rounds — 21 of them my materials-tier chain ids, which need promoting into the resource ledger, and 2 pre-existing (weaponParts, gunOil) that were never wired to a producer or a price.

The loop could not read its own verdicts

I wrote a critic schema and never passed it to the critic calls. Verdicts came back as prose, so "loop until it wins" degenerated into "always run three rounds" — which is most of why this cost fifty agents. The verdicts above were recovered from the transcripts afterwards.
