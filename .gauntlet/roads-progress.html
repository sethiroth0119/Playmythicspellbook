Read this first: what is not done

Every piece here was scored against a bar and passed it. That is not the same as finished, and it is definitely not the same as played. Nothing below has ever been touched by a human hand — every proof in this log came from a driven browser under Playwright. Nothing is committed, nothing is deployed, and no version knob was bumped.

Not done

  • No road is ever wider than one tile. A highway is darker, hatched and barriered — it is not wide. roads/mesh.js:52 refuses to touch RD_HW on purpose: moving it re-cuts every kerb, verge, footway, apron and junction in the city. The roundabout is nine one-tile roads, not a nine-tile object. So the network reads as nine surfaces, not as a hierarchy of capacities.
  • Power enforcement is opt-in, per city. enforce: true ships (power/tuning.js:567) but resolves as POWER.transmission.enforce && wired. A save written before this round has no wired key, comes back false, and behaves exactly as before. The constraint does not bite for any existing player until they press a button in the ⚡ panel that nobody has yet pressed by hand.
  • Water is the same shape. plumbed is derived: no pipe, no governed waterworks and no outfall ⇒ every factor is 1.00. A player who never touches the mains never feels the system exist.
  • The ocean was photographed at medium and low, not at potato. The harness itself degraded to potato mid-run (see the tier log in shots/ocean/report.json) — that tier is unmeasured.
  • No UI round. The road palette, the mains panel and the grid panel exist and print correct numbers; none of them has been through a legibility pass, and the road classes have no in-game explanation of what each one is for.

Deliberately cut

  • Multi-tile roads. Explicitly deferred to its own round — index.html:28378 already refused multi-tile occupancy once, at the Stadium, and shipped clearance instead.
  • Conversion priced as a difference. Re-laying a highway as an alley charges the full alley price, not the gap. A gap would be negative, and a negative charge mints Cinder outside the audited faucet — forbidden outright by ECONOMY.md. (roads/price.js:73)
  • Line loss in old cities. Computed and printed, never added to load, because a few per cent of extra demand tips a city that sits just above parity into a brownout it did not have yesterday.
  • Any SQL. Six pieces, zero migrations. Everything persists through the existing city blob — t.rc rides on the tile, pipes and lines go in the shelf. If you were expecting a /sql file to review, there isn't one.

A human must verify by playing

  • Does the drag feel like a drag? It was proved with synthesised pointer events at machine speed. The Browser pane composites at ~0.56 Hz; a real hand on a real mouse is the only thing that can say whether the preview quad keeps up.
  • Does the tool arbitration read? Zero zone cells painted during a road drag is asserted numerically in both directions. Whether a player notices that the other tool stood down — or just thinks the game ignored them — is unmeasured.
  • The one summary toast is one toast on a three-slot rail. Nobody has watched it land next to two other toasts.
  • The power opt-in is a live balance change to an existing save. Clicking it re-prices a city the player already built. That flow has never been played through once.
  • Does the mains panel now lead anywhere? The round-2 fix made the arithmetic per-component and the copy honest. Whether a human reads it and correctly concludes "join my two mains" is exactly the thing an assertion cannot check.
  • The sea on a real low-end device, at the tier that device actually boots at.

5 · Water and sewer as a real constraint

public/src/water/{network.js, netui.js} — 1,010 lines · 2 rounds · 0.72 → 8

The bar: the constraint must BITE, which is precisely where /src/power stopped. A consumer with no pipe path must tick measurably below its connected control in the SAME tick and recover when a run is dragged. The outfall refuses placement away from open water; the station refuses off-aquifer and names the aquifer. Pipes are a NETWORK: two disconnected runs are two components and only the one touching the station serves. A pre-existing save loads at factor 1.0 throughout.

RoundScoreVerdictWhat it did / what was left
10.72GAP Failed on the one assertion the bar names it by. The factor path aggregated over the WHOLE CITY where the connected component is the unit — one idea in two places:
· network.js:262 — reach = demandByComp[c] / totalDemand, a global denominator. Two properly plumbed districts, each with its own station, pipes and outfall, both ran at 0.650 while the same solve reported unserved: 0 and every consumer served: true.
· network.js:279/291/295 — load was city-total and capacity a flat sum over all outfalls, so two redundant outfalls on one component fully treated a disconnected component's sewage: backup 0.000, sewerFactor 1.000, bit-identical to the correct layout and below warnAbove, so nothing was said.
· The visible symptom was a self-contradicting panel: "100% of demand · every building reached" printed three lines above "the worst is at 65% … a run of pipe from it to the district it feeds is the whole fix" — advice that could not work, because the only remedy was joining the two mains, which the panel never said.
28PASS Gap closed by making both legs per-component. Reach is now computed BEFORE the factor, not after (network.js §3): a demand tile is reached when its component carries a waterworks, so a second district cannot take 35% off the first for the crime of existing. Sewer capacity is pooled per component and capped ONCE (§5), so two outfalls on one main can no longer treat a disconnected main's waste. Unreached demand is charged to the orthogonally nearest governed waterworks — the one whose district the building plainly belongs to, and therefore the one the player will drag from — which keeps the factor a gradient instead of collapsing to "are you touching a pipe, yes or no". An ungoverned Purifier is never charged an orphan.
unit of arithmetic: the component capacity capped once per component unplumbed city ⇒ every factor 1.00 pipes persist in the shelf: waterMains v1, cityId + 24 keys city id latched and checked
What the next round would attack The panel copy, now that the arithmetic underneath it is honest. The round-2 fix means the advice can be right; it does not prove a player reads it and joins their two mains. That needs a human, not an assertion — and it is the top item on the play-test list above.
