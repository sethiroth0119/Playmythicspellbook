The Freight Floor

What the carrier business looks like in play: a depot you plant, a fleet you buy off the Prince Portfolios auction floor, a public rate board other players hire you from, and freight on the road that can be taken off you.

These are mockups, not screenshots. Nothing renders yet — depot.render.js is one of the eight pieces still in the gauntlet, and the migration has to be pasted into the Supabase editor by hand before any of it has data behind it. The palette, fonts and component shapes here are lifted from the real game (index.html:95-131 and production.render.js), so this is what it will resemble — not a separate visual language invented for a pitch.

The halt banner is deliberately loud — that's not my choice, it's a rule the codebase already learned: "a halted building SAYS WHY, in the card, in words. The pip row is the whole rarity mechanic in one glance: solid = a run you still have, faded = spent today, dashed = a run condition took off you. Effective runs are floor(rarityRuns × PP_COND_MULT[condition]), so the Worn Rare above shows 5 pips with 2 dashed out. That's what makes a beaten Legendary a genuine choice against a Pristine Rare, using a multiplier table Prince Portfolios already has.

Meridian is a ceiling, not a competitor 2.5× the median player tariff and 1.6× the trip time, no escort, no illicit freight. It exists so a sole carrier can charge 2.4× and get rich, but cannot lock anyone out of the game.

Sorrel is the design working: cheapest quote, worst reliability, triple the risk. A carrier that undercuts on price and loses your cargo is a real option someone will take and regret, and reliability is the asset the careful carrier is actually building. Note the board sorts on nothing by default — the player picks what they're optimising.

🏴 Raided — 64 Fuel taken Hexcarrigan's crew hit the convoy 12 minutes out. 116 ⛽ delivered. Reliability on this route dropped to 91%. An escort would have cost ¢1,200 and cut risk to 6%.

The raided contract is the point of the whole feature. Freight that cannot be taken is just a delay timer — the risk is what makes escorts worth buying, territory worth holding, and reliability worth having. The toll line on CV-418 is one line of maths that gives Territory Wars a meaning for players who never fight.
